BETA PRIVACY NOTES / UPDATED AUGUST 20, 2026

Privacy in plain terms.

PageMe is early-access software. These notes describe the current beta behavior and will be replaced by the formal policy before a public store release.

Information kept on your phone

Your pager inbox, appearance choices, focus settings, selected study apps, emergency contact, and scheduling preferences are stored locally on the Android device. Android Auto Backup is disabled for this personal app state.

Information sent to the PageMe service

Account registration, account reauthentication, and PageMe-to-PageMe delivery use the PageMe backend. This includes the account details required to issue your UCN and the sender, recipient, and message information required to deliver a page.

Notification access

When you enable Notification Capture, PageMe reads eligible Android notifications so it can display them as pages, group conversations, and offer supported replies. The captured pager inbox is stored on the device. Weather, repeated alerts, PageMe's own notifications, and unsupported system notifications are filtered.

Optional permissions

  • Do Not Disturb access silences other alerts during Pager Mode.
  • Home-app access allows PageMe to become the launcher while Pager Mode is active.
  • Calendar access reads matching future events for focus reminders. PageMe does not edit or delete calendar events.
  • Emergency-contact setup uses Android's one-contact picker. PageMe receives only the contact you select and does not request access to your address book.
  • Camera permission is requested only when you press the torch button and is used solely to control the phone's rear flashlight.
  • Nearby-device access supports the built-in LoRa/Bluetooth receiver on compatible Android 12+ devices. PageMe does not request location access for this feature.

Your control

You can decline optional permissions, deactivate Pager Mode, restore your normal Home app, clear the pager inbox, or uninstall the beta from Android settings.

Beta caution

Install PageMe through the official Google Play testing link on this website. Do not install renamed copies from messages or third-party download sites.
