PRIVACY POLICY / UPDATED AUGUST 31, 2026

Privacy in plain terms.

This policy describes how the PageMe Android app handles information during its Google Play testing programme.

Information kept on your phone

Your pager inbox, appearance choices, focus settings, selected study apps, emergency contact, and scheduling preferences are stored locally on the Android device. Android Auto Backup is disabled for this personal app state.

Information sent to the PageMe service

Account registration, account reauthentication, and PageMe-to-PageMe delivery use the PageMe backend. This includes your name, email address, occupation, status, country, UCN, device messaging token, saved account settings, and the sender, recipient, and message information required to deliver a page. The service uses this information only to provide, secure, and support PageMe. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Notification access

When you enable Notification Capture, PageMe reads eligible Android notifications so it can display them as pages, group conversations, and offer supported replies. The captured pager inbox is stored on the device. Weather, repeated alerts, PageMe's own notifications, and unsupported system notifications are filtered.

Optional pager-status links

If you choose Share Status, PageMe creates a random, expiring link that lets another person open PageMe and address a page to you. The public status page does not display your name, UCN, email address, phone number, contacts, or message content. Status tokens are stored by the service only as cryptographic hashes and are revoked when the status ends, is replaced, or your account is deleted.

Limited product measurement

PageMe records a small set of first-party events needed to evaluate and operate the beta, such as activation, focus start or completion, status-link creation, link opening, opening Compose from a status link, and sending a page through that flow. These events use an internal account identifier and never contain page text, UCNs, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, captured notifications, selected apps, or calendar titles. Raw product events are retained for up to 90 days and are then aggregated or deleted. PageMe does not use third-party advertising analytics.

Optional permissions

  • Do Not Disturb access silences other alerts during Pager Mode.
  • Home-app access allows PageMe to become the launcher while Pager Mode is active.
  • Calendar access reads matching future events for focus reminders. PageMe does not edit or delete calendar events.
  • Emergency-contact setup uses Android's one-contact picker. PageMe receives only the contact you select and does not request access to your address book.
  • Camera permission is requested only when you press the torch button and is used solely to control the phone's rear flashlight.
  • Nearby-device access supports the built-in LoRa/Bluetooth receiver on compatible Android 12+ devices. PageMe does not request location access for this feature.

Your control

You can decline optional permissions, deactivate Pager Mode, restore your normal Home app, clear the pager inbox, or uninstall the beta from Android settings.

Account and data deletion

To permanently delete your PageMe account, open PageMe, go to Settings, select Delete account, choose Delete account again, and confirm. This removes the account profile, PageMe network messages, registered devices, active sessions, blocks, reports, status links, and product events associated with the account from the PageMe service. PageMe also clears the account and pager data stored by the app on that phone.

If you cannot access the app, email Palmartech18@gmail.com from your registered email address and include your UCN to request account deletion.

Security and retention

PageMe uses encrypted HTTPS connections when communicating with its service. UCN pages are not described as end-to-end encrypted. PageMe network messages are retained for up to 90 days unless a user clears them or deletes the account sooner. Raw product events are retained for up to 90 days. Account and session records remain while the account is active and are removed when the account is deleted.

Beta caution

Install PageMe through the official Google Play testing link on this website. Do not install renamed copies from messages or third-party download sites.
