### Description

PageMe turns the Android phone you already own into a focused pager. Calls, SMS, WhatsApp and selected notifications become grouped pages, with supported replies that avoid distracting feeds. Every user also gets a UCN, a PageMe address for sending direct pages to other PageMe users without sharing a phone number. Timers, schedules and calendar events can activate Pager Mode while approved study apps and emergency access remain available.

## First maker comment

Hi Product Hunt,

I built PageMe because silencing distracting apps did not change the fact that they were still one swipe away. PageMe changes the phone's context instead: with permission, it becomes a focused pager interface where important communication remains available without putting feeds, reels, and recommendations back in front of you.

Calls, SMS, WhatsApp, and selected notifications become grouped pages with supported replies. Each registered user also receives a Unique Code Number, or UCN, which works as a PageMe address for direct pages between PageMe users without exchanging phone numbers.

PageMe can start through a focus timer, a saved schedule, or matching calendar events. Approved study apps, alarms, trusted contacts, and a controlled emergency exit remain available because focus still needs to be practical.

The public beta is free. I would especially value honest feedback on three things: whether important communication stays reachable, whether Pager Mode creates enough separation from distracting apps, and where setup or Android permissions still feel unclear.

Thank you for trying PageMe.
