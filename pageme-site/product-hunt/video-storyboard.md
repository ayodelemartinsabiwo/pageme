# PageMe Product Hunt demo storyboard

Target duration: 60-75 seconds. Upload the finished video to YouTube as unlisted and use the full YouTube URL.

| Time | Picture | On-screen message |
|---|---|---|
| 0-6s | Android Home changes into the PageMe pager | Your Android. Now a focused pager. |
| 6-17s | Inbox categories, grouped sender, and a supported reply | Keep the message. Leave the feed. |
| 17-32s | Two fictional accounts, AMI-204 and BEN-001, exchange a UCN page on separate devices | Page people directly by Unique Code Number. |
| 32-41s | Delivered/read state and grouped UCN conversation | One conversation. Exactly-once delivery. |
| 41-53s | Focus timer starts; only approved Study Apps remain available | Focus that changes the environment. |
| 53-63s | Schedule and matching calendar event activate Pager Mode | Focus can arrive on schedule. |
| 63-72s | Alarm controls, trusted contact, emergency exit, then PageMe returns | Strict enough to help. Safe enough to use. |
| 72-75s | PageMe mark and Google Play badge | Make the phone smaller. |

Record all mobile footage from the public candidate build. Use fictional accounts and hide owner information, notification shade content, email addresses, phone numbers, device identifiers, and status-bar details. Do not describe UCN delivery as end-to-end encrypted.
