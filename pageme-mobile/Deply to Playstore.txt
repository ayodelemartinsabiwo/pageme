### Step 4: Distribution Options

#### Option A: Publish on the Google Play Store (AAB)
1. Sign up for a [Google Play Developer Console](https://play.google.com/console) account (one-time $25 fee).
2. Create a new app, upload your generated `.aab` file to the **Internal Testing** or **Production** track.
3. Submit the listing details, screenshots, and complete the target audience questionnaire.
4. Google will review and publish the app directly onto the Play Store for your users.

#### Option B: Direct APK Distribution (Ad-Hoc)
1. Upload the signed `.apk` file to a download link on your website or repository releases.
2. Direct users to open the link on their phones.
3. Because PageMe is a custom home launcher, Android will prompt users to enable **"Install unknown apps"** in their settings to sideload the APK.
4. Once installed, users can select PageMe as their default home screen.
